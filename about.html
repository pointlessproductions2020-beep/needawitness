About Need A Witness UK

Who We Are

Need A Witness UK was created to solve a simple but stressful problem: finding reliable wedding witnesses at short notice. Whether your ceremony is tomorrow, next week, or months away, we connect you with trustworthy witnesses who are ready to attend and support your special moment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make weddings easier, calmer, and more accessible for everyone. No couple should ever feel stressed or embarrassed because they don’t have witnesses available. We’re here to make sure your ceremony goes ahead smoothly and without worry.

Why We Started

Many couples face unexpected challenges when planning a wedding — especially when it comes to witnesses. Friends may be unavailable, family may live far away, or you may simply want a private ceremony. We saw how common this issue was and built a service that solves it quickly, professionally, and affordably.

What We Do

We provide:

  • Reliable, verified wedding witnesses
  • Fast bookings for last‑minute ceremonies
  • Professional and friendly service
  • Coverage across Kent and surrounding areas
  • Simple online booking and secure payments

Our Witnesses

Our witnesses are everyday people who enjoy helping couples on their big day. Each witness is verified, punctual, respectful, and understands the importance of their role in your ceremony.

Our Values

  • Reliability: We show up on time, every time.
  • Professionalism: We treat every ceremony with respect.
  • Inclusivity: All couples are welcome.
  • Privacy: Your information is always protected.
  • Support: We’re here to help, not complicate things.

Business Hours & Pricing Transparency

We operate Monday to Saturday at our standard rates. Sunday ceremonies are available with a premium surcharge, and UK Bank Holidays are charged at double the standard rate. Deposits are adjusted accordingly, so you always know the cost before you commit.
