No‑Show Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what happens when a witness or customer fails to attend a confirmed booking with Need A Witness UK. It should be read alongside our Customer Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy.

1. Our commitment

We aim to provide reliable, professional witnesses at the agreed venue and time. We will exercise reasonable care and skill in arranging and delivering the agreed service.

2. Witness arrangements

We may arrange attendance through self-employed independent contractors. Where we use contractors, this does not remove our responsibilities to customers under their booking agreement or applicable consumer law.

3. If a witness is running late

We will make reasonable efforts to contact the witness, keep you informed and arrange a suitable replacement if necessary and practicable. Please contact us promptly if your witness has not arrived at the agreed time.

4. If a witness does not arrive

  • We will attempt to arrange a suitable replacement where possible.
  • If we cannot provide the agreed service and the ceremony cannot proceed as a result, we will refund the full amount paid to us for that booking.
  • If only part of the agreed service is delivered, we will assess an appropriate refund or price reduction, taking account of whether the service fulfilled its agreed purpose and your statutory rights.
  • We will review the circumstances and take appropriate action concerning the witness.

5. Customer no-shows and same-day cancellations

If you fail to attend, cancel on the day, or the ceremony cannot proceed for reasons attributable to you, we may retain a reasonable amount from payments received or seek payment of a reasonable outstanding cancellation charge in accordance with our Customer Terms and Refund Policy.

Where witnesses have already travelled or arrived, the charge may be up to the full agreed booking price if justified by our actual net loss. A full charge is not automatic. We take account of reasonable preparation costs, committed witness fees, travel costs, lost booking income where justified, costs saved and any sums recovered through alternative bookings, without double counting.

We will refund any amount paid in excess of the justified charge. We may also offer a discretionary goodwill refund. No separate mileage or parking charge will be added to a fixed venue booking merely because a cancellation occurs.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Provide accurate ceremony dates, times and venue details.
  • Notify us promptly of changes, delays or cancellations.
  • Remain reachable on the day and attend at the agreed time.
  • Follow the venue and registrar's requirements for the ceremony.

7. Refunds and liability

Refunds and any other remedies will be assessed in accordance with the booking agreement and applicable law. We do not automatically reimburse venue fees, registrar fees or customer travel expenses; however, nothing in this policy excludes any entitlement to compensation or other remedy under applicable law.

Where an event outside our reasonable control prevents attendance, we will explain the available options and assess any refund due. Nothing in this policy excludes or restricts liability where doing so would be unlawful, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or breach of statutory consumer rights.

8. Updates to this policy

The version agreed at the time of booking applies to that booking, subject to statutory rights and any subsequent changes mutually agreed with the customer.
