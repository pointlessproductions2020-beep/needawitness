These Terms & Conditions govern use of the Need A Witness UK platform (“the Service”). Customer bookings are also governed by our Customer Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy. Witness participation is subject to the Witness Agreement. Nothing in these terms limits statutory rights.

2. Our Service

Need A Witness UK arranges independent witnesses for ceremonies and manages bookings, communication and payments. We are not a legal authority and do not provide legal advice. Witnesses attend to perform the agreed witnessing service, subject to the requirements of the relevant ceremony officials. Using independent witnesses does not remove our contractual responsibilities to customers.

3. User Accounts

Users must provide accurate information when creating an account. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login details and for all activity under your account.

4. Witness Responsibilities

Witnesses agree to:

  • Arrive on time to confirmed bookings
  • Act professionally and respectfully at all times
  • Follow all instructions from register office, venue and ceremony staff
  • Only sign documents they are legally permitted to sign
  • Notify Need A Witness UK immediately if they cannot attend

5. Couple Responsibilities

Couples agree to:

  • Provide accurate ceremony details
  • Ensure witnesses are treated respectfully
  • Notify Need A Witness UK of any changes or cancellations
  • Pay all fees associated with the booking

6. Payments

Payments are processed through Stripe or Monzo. A wedding booking is secured when we confirm availability and receive the agreed deposit, or expressly waive it in writing. The standard deposit is £50 and forms part of the total price. The remaining balance is due at least 24 hours before the ceremony unless otherwise agreed in writing. Short-notice bookings normally require full payment upon confirmation.

7. Pricing Transparency

Wedding witness prices start at £300 for one witness and £350 total for two witnesses. Fixed venue prices include ordinary travel and parking; there is no separate mileage charge. Sunday bookings incur a £50 surcharge. Applicable bank holidays are charged at twice the venue service price instead of the Sunday surcharge. Bookings made less than 24 hours before the ceremony incur a £50 short-notice surcharge after any bank holiday multiplier. Unlisted venues require an individual quotation. Alternative prices, deposits and surcharges may be agreed in writing. The total including applicable taxes and compulsory charges is disclosed before payment commitment. Document witnessing and other separately quoted services may have different prices.

8. Cancellations & Refunds

Please notify us promptly in writing of cancellations or changes. Subject to statutory rights, cancellation charges reflect reasonable net losses, including committed witness fees, preparation and travel, less costs saved and replacement bookings. We take reasonable steps to reduce losses. Same-day cancellations may justify a charge up to the full agreed price, but this is not automatic. Deposits are not automatically forfeited. Any excess payment will be refunded. If a witness cancels, we will make reasonable efforts to arrange a suitable replacement at no additional cost. If we cannot supply the agreed service, applicable refund rights will be honoured, including a full refund where the service cannot be supplied. See our Refund Policy for details, including statutory cancellation rights.
