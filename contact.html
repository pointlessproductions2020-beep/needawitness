Business Hours & Pricing Notes

We operate 6 days a week (Monday to Saturday) at our standard rates. Sunday ceremonies are available with a premium surcharge, and UK Bank Holidays are charged at double the standard rate. This ensures fair compensation for witnesses working outside normal hours and on national holidays.
