Witness Code of Conduct

Last updated: [add date]

All witnesses on the Need A Witness UK platform must follow this Code of Conduct. It ensures professionalism, reliability, and a positive experience for couples.

1. Professional Behaviour

  • Be respectful, polite, and calm at all times
  • Dress appropriately for a wedding ceremony
  • Follow venue rules and registrar instructions
  • Maintain confidentiality and discretion

2. Punctuality

  • Arrive at least 10–15 minutes before the ceremony
  • Plan travel to avoid delays
  • Notify us immediately if you are running late

3. Communication

  • Respond promptly to messages about bookings
  • Be reachable on the day of the ceremony
  • Inform us immediately of any issues or emergencies

4. Reliability

  • Only accept jobs you can confidently attend
  • No‑shows without notice may result in removal from the platform
  • Repeated lateness may also lead to suspension

5. Legal Requirements

  • You must be over 16 (local authority rules may vary)
  • You must understand the ceremony and declarations
  • You must speak and understand English clearly
  • You must be able to sign your name on the marriage schedule

6. Conduct at the Ceremony

  • Follow registrar instructions
  • Do not disrupt the ceremony
  • No alcohol or intoxication before or during the job
  • Be respectful to all guests and staff

7. Platform Rules

  • Do not contact couples directly outside the platform
  • Do not negotiate prices privately
  • Do not share personal data unnecessarily

8. Removal From Platform

We may suspend or remove witnesses for:

  • unprofessional behaviour
  • no‑shows or repeated lateness
  • misuse of the platform
  • providing false information
