Witness Code of Conduct

Last updated: September 2026

All witnesses using the Need A Witness UK platform are expected to follow this Code of Conduct. It is intended to support professionalism, reliability, discretion, and a positive experience for customers, registrars, ceremony officials, venues, and other attendees.

1. Professional Behaviour

  • Be respectful, polite, calm, and discreet at all times
  • Dress appropriately for the ceremony and make reasonable efforts to follow any customer dress-code preference provided for the booking
  • Follow lawful instructions given by registrars, ceremony officials, and venue staff
  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries
  • Keep customer and booking information confidential except where disclosure is necessary to fulfil the booking or required by law

2. Punctuality

  • Plan to arrive in good time before the ceremony, normally at least 10–15 minutes early unless otherwise advised
  • Plan your travel appropriately and allow for foreseeable delays
  • Notify Need A Witness UK immediately if you are running late or encounter a problem that may affect attendance

3. Communication

  • Respond promptly to important messages relating to accepted bookings
  • Remain reasonably reachable on the day of an accepted booking
  • Inform Need A Witness UK as soon as possible about any issue, emergency, or change that may affect your ability to attend

4. Reliability

  • Only accept bookings you reasonably expect to be able to attend
  • Once you accept a booking, make every reasonable effort to fulfil it
  • Unexplained no-shows or serious/repeated late cancellations may result in suspension or removal from the platform
  • Repeated lateness may also lead to suspension or removal

5. Ceremony Requirements

Requirements for acting as a witness can depend on the type of ceremony, registrar or ceremony official, venue requirements, and applicable law. You must only accept and attend a booking where you are capable of acting as a witness for that ceremony.

  • You must be able to understand what you are witnessing
  • You must be able to follow the instructions of the registrar or ceremony official
  • You must be able to provide the information and signature reasonably required for the ceremony documentation
  • You must tell Need A Witness UK promptly if you become aware of any reason you may not be able to act as a witness

6. Conduct at the Ceremony

  • Follow the reasonable and lawful instructions of the registrar, ceremony official, and venue staff
  • Do not disrupt the ceremony or interfere with the couple, guests, or staff
  • Do not attend while impaired by alcohol or drugs
  • Be respectful to customers, guests, registrars, ceremony officials, and venue staff
  • Do not take photographs, video, or audio recordings unless specifically requested or clearly permitted

7. Platform & Customer Information

  • Do not use customer contact details for unrelated personal or commercial purposes
  • Do not privately negotiate alternative witness-service arrangements arising from a platform booking
  • Do not share personal or booking information unnecessarily
  • Use customer information only as reasonably necessary to fulfil the accepted booking

8. Suspension or Removal From the Platform

Need A Witness UK may suspend or remove access to the platform where reasonably necessary, including for:

  • serious or repeated unprofessional behaviour
  • no-shows, repeated lateness, or repeated late cancellations
  • misuse of customer information or the platform
  • providing materially false or misleading information
  • conduct that creates a reasonable safety, legal, or reputational concern
