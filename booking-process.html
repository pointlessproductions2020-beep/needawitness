Booking & Payment Process

Last updated: [add date]

This page explains how bookings and payments work when using Need A Witness UK. Our process is designed to be simple, secure, and transparent for all couples.

1. Submit a Booking Request

Complete our online booking form with your ceremony details. This creates a booking request, not a confirmed booking.

2. We Review Your Request

We check witness availability for your:

  • date
  • time
  • location
  • witness requirements

If we can cover your ceremony, we will approve your request.

3. Pay the £20 Non‑Refundable Booking Fee

Once approved, we will send you a secure payment link (Monzo/Stripe). This £20 fee:

  • secures your date
  • covers administration and allocation
  • is non‑refundable

4. Booking Confirmation

Your booking is officially confirmed once the £20 fee has been paid.

5. Remaining Balance

The remaining balance must be paid no later than 48 hours before the ceremony.

If payment is not received by this time:

  • the booking will be cancelled
  • the £20 booking fee is non‑refundable
  • witnesses will be released from the booking

6. Witness Attendance

Once fully paid, your allocated witness (or witness team) will attend your ceremony at the agreed time and location.

7. Payment to Witnesses

Witnesses are paid by us after the ceremony. All witnesses are independent self‑employed contractors.
