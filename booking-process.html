Booking & Payment Process

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains how bookings and payments work with Need A Witness UK. Our fixed venue pricing makes the process simple and transparent.

1. Submit a Booking Request

Choose your ceremony date, time, venue and number of witnesses through our online booking process. You will see the applicable price before submitting your request. This is a booking request, not a confirmed booking.

If your venue is not listed, contact us for an individually agreed quotation.

2. We Review Your Request

We check your ceremony date and time, approved ceremony venue, witness requirements, availability and any dress-code or accessibility requests. If we can cover your ceremony, we will contact you to arrange confirmation and payment.

3. Pay Your Deposit

Once approved, we will send you payment instructions. The standard deposit is £50 per booking, regardless of the number of witnesses or day of the ceremony, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Your deposit counts towards the total price. Refunds are governed by our Refund Policy and your statutory rights.

4. Booking Confirmation

Your booking is secured once we have confirmed availability and received the agreed deposit, or confirmed alternative payment arrangements in writing.

5. Remaining Balance

The remaining balance must be paid no later than 24 hours before the ceremony, unless otherwise agreed in writing. For bookings made within 24 hours, full payment is normally required upon confirmation.

If payment is not received by the agreed deadline, we may cancel the booking and release the witnesses. Any cancellation charge or refund will be assessed under our customer terms and refund policy.

6. Witness Attendance

Once the agreed payment requirements have been met, your allocated witness or witnesses will attend at the agreed time and location. Please notify us promptly of any changes to your ceremony arrangements.

7. Payment to Witnesses

We arrange payment to our witnesses directly. Customers do not need to pay witnesses separately.

8. Fixed Venue Pricing

  • One professional witness: from £300
  • Two professional witnesses: from £350 total
  • Sunday ceremonies: £50 surcharge
  • Applicable bank holidays: double the venue service price, without an additional Sunday surcharge
  • Short-notice bookings within 24 hours: £50 surcharge

Prices vary by venue. Applicable surcharges are shown before submission. Individually agreed quotations, discounts and payment arrangements may differ from standard online prices. Existing confirmed bookings retain their agreed prices and terms.

9. Travel and Parking Included

We have replaced mileage-based pricing with fixed venue prices. Your agreed price includes witness preparation, travel and parking for the agreed ceremony arrangements, so you know the cost in advance without a separate mileage charge.

10. Simple, Professional Service

Need A Witness UK provides professional ceremony witnesses without requiring customers to purchase photographs, video packages, confetti or other add-ons. You can include a preferred dress code or presentation request with your booking.
