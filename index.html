How It Works

We keep things simple, professional, and stress‑free:

  1. Check our availability and choose a time.
  2. Fill out the booking form on our secure booking page.
  3. We confirm availability for your ceremony.
  4. You choose your preferred payment method.
  5. Once paid, your booking is secured.
  6. We attend your registry office, sign as witnesses, and take photos if requested.

FAQ

Do you attend wedding venues?

No — we attend registry offices only for signatures and optional photos.

Do you take photos?

Yes — we can take photos on your phone for free, or you can choose our photo & video add‑on.

How do I pay?

You can pay via PayPal, Stripe, or bank transfer.

Is my booking confirmed immediately?

Your booking is confirmed once payment is received.

Do Sundays or Bank Holidays cost more?

Yes. We operate Monday to Saturday at our standard rates. Sunday bookings include a premium surcharge, and UK Bank Holidays are charged at double the standard rate.
