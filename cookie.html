1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Need A Witness UK (“we”, “our”, “the Service”) may use cookies and similar technologies on our website.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website. They can be used to help websites function correctly, remember preferences, improve security, and understand how a website is being used.

3. Types of Cookies and Similar Technologies

3.1 Essential Technologies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be necessary for parts of the website or Witness Hub to function, including features such as:

  • authentication and login sessions
  • security and fraud prevention
  • basic website and account functionality

3.2 Functional Technologies

These may be used to remember information or preferences that improve how the website works for you, such as session state or user settings.

3.3 Analytics and Performance

We may use analytics or performance tools to understand how visitors use the website and to help us identify technical issues or improve the service. Where non-essential cookies require consent, they should only be used in accordance with applicable requirements.

3.4 Third-Party Services

Some features of the website rely on third-party services for functions such as authentication, data storage, communications, payments, hosting, or security. Those providers may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies according to their own policies where applicable.

4. Managing Cookies

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • block or allow cookies
  • block third-party cookies
  • delete existing cookies and site data
  • change permissions for individual websites

Disabling technologies that are necessary for authentication, security, or core website functionality may prevent some parts of the Service from working correctly.

5. Privacy

For more information about how we collect, use, store, and share personal information, please see our Privacy Policy.
