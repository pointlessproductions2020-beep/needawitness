Cookie Policy

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Need A Witness UK (“we”, “our”, “the Service”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. By using our site, you agree to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly, improve performance, and provide insights into how visitors use the platform.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function. They enable features such as:

  • Logging into your account
  • Security and authentication
  • Basic site navigation

3.2 Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve the experience. They may collect:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the site
  • Browser and device type

We may use tools such as Google Analytics or privacy‑focused alternatives. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics cookies.

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the site to remember your preferences, such as:

  • Saved login state
  • Form inputs
  • User settings

3.4 Third‑Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third‑party services we use, such as:

  • Stripe (for secure payments)
  • Firebase (for authentication and security)
  • Analytics tools

4. How to Manage Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third‑party cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Receive alerts when cookies are used

Please note: disabling essential cookies may prevent parts of the website from functioning.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Continued use of the site indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
