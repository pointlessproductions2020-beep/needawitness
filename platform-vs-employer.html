Platform and Independent Witnesses

Last updated: September 2026

Need A Witness UK operates as an introductory and booking platform connecting customers with independent self-employed witnesses. This page explains the basis on which witnesses use the platform and how the service is structured.

1. Independent Witnesses

Witnesses accepting bookings through the platform do so on a self-employed, independent basis. They are responsible for matters associated with their self-employed activity, including:

  • their own tax and National Insurance obligations
  • managing their availability
  • deciding whether to accept available bookings
  • their conduct and punctuality when fulfilling an accepted booking
  • their own arrangements for travelling to bookings

2. How the Platform Operates

Witnesses are not required to work fixed hours or accept a minimum number of bookings. They may make themselves available when they choose and decide whether to accept work offered through the platform.

Need A Witness UK does not require exclusivity, and witnesses may provide services elsewhere. Standards relating to professionalism, conduct, confidentiality, punctuality, and the proper fulfilment of an accepted booking are intended to protect customers and the integrity of the service.

3. Our Role

Customers book the witness service rather than a guaranteed named individual. Need A Witness UK facilitates the booking process by:

  • receiving and managing booking requests
  • administering payments
  • matching or allocating available witnesses
  • providing booking-related customer support

4. Employment Status

Employment status under UK law depends on the reality of the working relationship and the relevant legal tests; it is not determined solely by the label used in an agreement or on this website. The platform is structured for witnesses to provide services independently rather than as employees.

5. Replacements, No-Shows and Liability

A booking does not guarantee attendance by a particular named witness. If an assigned witness becomes unavailable, Need A Witness UK may attempt to arrange a suitable replacement.

Refunds, replacements, and limitations of liability are governed by the applicable Customer Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy, No-Show Policy, and Liability Disclaimer. Nothing on this page excludes or limits any right or liability where it cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

6. Flexibility

The platform model is designed to allow customers to request professional witness services while giving independent witnesses flexibility over when they make themselves available and which bookings they accept.
