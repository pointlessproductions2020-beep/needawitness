Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains refunds and cancellation charges for Need A Witness UK. Read it alongside our Customer Terms & Conditions. Nothing in this policy limits your statutory consumer rights.

1. Booking payments

The standard wedding booking deposit is £50 and forms part of the agreed total, including travel and parking. The remaining balance is due at least 24 hours before the ceremony unless otherwise agreed in writing. Short-notice bookings normally require full payment upon confirmation. Individually agreed deposits and prices, and separately quoted services, may differ. Deposits are not automatically non-refundable.

2. Customer cancellations

Please notify us in writing as soon as possible. Subject to statutory rights, cancellation charges reflect reasonable net losses directly resulting from cancellation, including committed witness fees, preparation and travel costs. We take reasonable steps to reduce losses and account for costs saved and replacement bookings.

  • More than 24 hours before the ceremony: We refund payments less any justified cancellation charge.
  • Within 24 hours of the ceremony: Charges may be higher because witness commitments and travel arrangements may already be in place.
  • On the day of the ceremony: Where witnesses have travelled, arrived or committed their time, the charge may be up to the full agreed booking price if justified by actual net loss. A full charge is not automatic.

Any payment above the applicable charge will be refunded. We will explain the charge on request. We may also offer discretionary goodwill refunds, without affecting your legal rights.

3. Statutory cancellation rights

Where a statutory cooling-off period applies, we will honour it. If you expressly request performance during that period, any lawful charge for services already supplied will be proportionate. Loss of the cancellation right upon full performance requires the consent and acknowledgement required by law. These rights take precedence over our ordinary cancellation charges.

4. Cancellations by Need A Witness UK

If a witness becomes unavailable, we will make reasonable efforts to arrange a suitable replacement. If we cannot supply the agreed service, you are entitled to the applicable refund, including a full refund of all payments for that service where it cannot be supplied. Any claim for additional losses will be assessed in accordance with applicable law.

5. Witness no-shows and late arrivals

Please contact us immediately if a witness is late or does not arrive. We will attempt to resolve the issue, including arranging a replacement where possible. Where the agreed service cannot be provided, we will honour applicable refund rights. If a late witness still performs the service, any refund or price reduction will depend on the circumstances and your statutory rights. We may also offer a goodwill refund.

6. Incorrect information or customer no-show

If the service cannot be supplied because of incorrect ceremony details, undisclosed access restrictions, a customer no-show or changes not communicated to us, any cancellation charge will be assessed on the same reasonable net-loss basis described above, subject to statutory rights.

7. Processing refunds

Refunds will be made to the original payment method where possible, without undue delay and within any applicable statutory deadline. Once issued, funds may take 3–10 working days to appear depending on your payment provider. We may make reasonable checks where fraud is suspected, without restricting statutory refund rights.

8. Existing bookings

Existing confirmed bookings retain their agreed prices and the terms applicable when booked, subject to statutory rights.

9. Contact

To cancel a booking or request a refund, email needawitnessuk@gmail.com or use our contact page. Please include your booking details.
