Witness Onboarding Guide

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Need A Witness UK. This guide explains the practical basics of using the platform as an independent witness, including how bookings work, what is expected at ceremonies, and how payments and communication are handled.

1. Your Role as a Witness

Your role is to attend an accepted ceremony booking and act as a witness where you are capable of doing so. Requirements can vary depending on the type of ceremony, registrar or ceremony official, venue, and applicable law.

  • you must be able to understand what you are witnessing
  • you must be able to follow the instructions of the registrar or ceremony official
  • you must be able to provide the information and signature reasonably required for the ceremony documentation
  • you must tell Need A Witness UK promptly if you become aware of any reason you may not be able to act as a witness

2. Independent Working Arrangement

Witnesses use the platform on a self-employed, independent basis. In practice, this means:

  • you choose which available jobs to accept
  • you manage your own availability
  • you are responsible for your own tax and National Insurance obligations
  • you are not required to work exclusively through Need A Witness UK

Employment status under UK law depends on the reality of the working relationship and applicable legal tests. The platform is structured for witnesses to provide services independently.

3. How Bookings Work

The usual process is:

  • A customer submits a booking request
  • Need A Witness UK identifies suitable available witnesses
  • You receive the relevant booking details, such as date, time, venue, and any customer notes
  • You can accept or decline the job
  • If accepted, you attend the ceremony and fulfil the witness role professionally
  • Payment is arranged after the completed booking in line with the agreed payout for that job

4. Professional Expectations

We expect all witnesses to:

  • arrive in good time, normally at least 10–15 minutes before the ceremony unless otherwise advised
  • dress appropriately and make reasonable efforts to follow any dress-code preference provided for the booking
  • behave respectfully, professionally, and discreetly
  • follow lawful instructions from registrars, ceremony officials, and venue staff
  • communicate promptly if any issue may affect the booking
  • keep customer and booking information confidential

5. Travel

You are responsible for planning your own travel to and from accepted bookings unless a specific arrangement says otherwise. Allow enough time for foreseeable delays and contact Need A Witness UK immediately if you believe you may be late.

Any travel payment, mileage contribution, or reimbursement due to you will be shown or agreed as part of the relevant booking or payout arrangement.

6. No-Shows & Cancellations

Once you accept a booking, you are expected to make every reasonable effort to attend. If an emergency or other problem means you cannot attend, notify Need A Witness UK as soon as possible so that a replacement can be sought.

Repeated late cancellations, unexplained absences, or no-shows may result in suspension or removal from the platform.

7. Payments

Payments are made for completed bookings based on the payout shown or agreed for the job, subject to any applicable platform terms or deductions disclosed to you.

Payments are sent using the payment details you provide. You are responsible for keeping those details accurate and for dealing with any tax, National Insurance, or other obligations relating to your income.

8. Communication

You should remain reasonably reachable on the day of an accepted booking and respond promptly to important messages relating to it. If we cannot contact you and there is a reasonable concern that the booking may not be fulfilled, we may seek a replacement witness.

9. Customer Information & Privacy

Customer contact and booking information must only be used as reasonably necessary to fulfil an accepted booking. Do not use customer details for unrelated personal or commercial purposes, and do not share them unnecessarily.

Need A Witness UK processes witness information for platform administration, booking allocation, communications, payments, support, and safety in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection law.

10. Suspension or Removal From the Platform

Access to the platform may be suspended or removed where reasonably necessary, including for:

  • serious or repeated unprofessional behaviour
  • no-shows, repeated lateness, or repeated late cancellations
  • misuse of customer information or the platform
  • providing materially false or misleading information
  • conduct that creates a reasonable safety, legal, or reputational concern

11. Before Your First Booking

Before accepting your first job, make sure your contact, availability, and payment details are accurate, and read the Witness Agreement and Witness Code of Conduct. If a booking includes a dress-code preference, venue instruction, or other important note, review it before travelling.

12. Acceptance

By registering as a witness and using the platform, you confirm that you understand your responsibilities and agree to follow the Witness Agreement, Witness Code of Conduct, and other applicable platform policies.
