What happens next?
We usually review new witnesses within 24 hours. Once approved, you’ll get full access to your dashboard, jobs, availability, and profile tools.
Optional uploads to speed up approval

These are completely optional, but they can help us approve you faster and with more confidence. You can also skip this step and we’ll review your application based on the details you already provided.

Photo of your ID (optional)
A clear photo of your driving licence, passport, or other official ID. You can blur sensitive numbers if you prefer, as long as your name and photo are visible.
Selfie holding your ID (optional)
A quick selfie holding your ID next to your face. This helps us confirm you match the ID.
Profile photo (optional)
This is the photo couples may eventually see on your profile. A clear, friendly headshot works best.
