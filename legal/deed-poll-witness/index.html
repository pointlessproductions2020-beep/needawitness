Unenrolled deed polls

Who can witness a deed poll?

For the standard GOV.UK DIY unenrolled deed poll process, the two witnesses must be aged 18 or over. GOV.UK also warns that some organisations may refuse a deed poll where a witness lives at the same address as you or is a close relative.

That makes an independent witnessing service particularly useful: you can arrange adults who are attending specifically to witness the signing rather than relying on family or somebody in your household.

Important distinction: this service is for ordinary deed poll witnessing. An enrolled deed poll involves additional court forms and some of those documents require a solicitor or another authorised witness. We do not provide statutory declaration, solicitor, commissioner-for-oaths or court-officer services.

Questions

Deed poll witnessing FAQs

How many witnesses do I need for an unenrolled deed poll?

GOV.UK's DIY unenrolled deed poll guidance says you need two witnesses to sign it, and they must be 18 or over. Our deed poll service therefore defaults to two witnesses.

Can my family witness my deed poll?

GOV.UK says some organisations may not accept a deed poll where a witness lives at the same address or is a close relative. Using independent witnesses can avoid that practical issue, but you should still check what the organisations receiving your deed poll will accept.

Do you make the deed poll for me?

No. We provide independent witnesses for a document that is already prepared. GOV.UK provides information about making your own unenrolled deed poll, and solicitors or specialist agencies can also prepare deed polls.

Can you witness an enrolled deed poll?

Parts of the enrolled process use two ordinary witnesses, but other forms require a solicitor or another authorised witness. Because the process is different, contact us before booking if your request relates to an enrolled deed poll. We do not witness statutory declarations in the capacity of a solicitor or commissioner for oaths.

Will every organisation accept my unenrolled deed poll?

Not necessarily. GOV.UK advises checking with the organisation you are dealing with because some may require an enrolled deed poll instead.

Can you come to my home or office?

Yes. Enter the signing location and postcode when booking and the system will calculate the return mileage charge before you submit the request.
