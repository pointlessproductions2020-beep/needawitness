What does witnessing a deed involve?

For an individual executing a deed in the usual way, the signature is made in the presence of a witness who attests it. For deeds submitted to HM Land Registry, current guidance says the witness must be physically present when the deed is signed.

The witness details may form an important part of the execution. HM Land Registry guidance expects the witness's signature, name and full address to be recorded clearly where its requirements apply.

Your deed decides the job. Different deeds, signatories and transactions can have different execution requirements. We provide the independent witness you request; we do not decide how your deed should be executed, draft the execution clause or provide legal advice. Deed witnessing FAQs

How many witnesses does my deed need?

That depends on the deed and how it is being executed. Many individual signatures are attested by one witness, but some situations require a different process. Check your deed's execution instructions or ask the professional handling it. Our booking system allows one or two witnesses.

Can the same witness witness more than one signature?

In some circumstances, yes, but each required signature still needs to be properly attested and the deed's execution wording matters. If several people are signing, confirm the required process before booking.

Can you witness a property or Land Registry deed?

Potentially, where the document requires an ordinary witness and you have confirmed its execution requirements. We also have a dedicated Property Document Witnessing service for property-related requests.

Can you witness over a video call?

Our service is in person. For deeds within HM Land Registry's ordinary witnessing requirements, its current guidance requires the witness to be physically present when the deed is signed.

Can you check whether my deed has been completed correctly?

No. We witness signatures; we do not review the legal effect of the deed, draft clauses or provide legal advice. Ask the solicitor, conveyancer or document provider if you need the document checked.

Can you come to my home or office?

Yes. Enter the signing address when booking and the system will calculate the travel charge before you submit your request.
