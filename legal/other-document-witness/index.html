What this service covers

Ordinary signature witnessing

This service is intended for documents where the person or organisation requiring the paperwork has confirmed that an ordinary independent witness is acceptable. That may include certain agreements, consent forms or other documents containing a conventional witness section.

Because document requirements vary enormously, we ask you to tell us what the document is when booking. If the role requires a solicitor, notary public, commissioner for oaths, authorised person or somebody with a particular profession or personal knowledge of you, our ordinary witness service is not a substitute.

Unsure? That's fine. Submit the document type and any witnessing instructions with your request. We can determine whether the job fits the service we provide — but we cannot advise you on the legal validity of the document or decide what form of witnessing the law or receiving organisation requires.

Questions

Other document witnessing FAQs

What kinds of documents can you witness?

Potentially any prepared document where an ordinary independent adult is permitted to witness the signature. Because requirements vary, tell us the document type and any instructions when booking so the request can be checked for suitability.

Can you witness a statutory declaration?

Not through this service. Statutory declarations must be made before a person authorised to administer them under the applicable rules. Our ordinary independent witness service does not claim that authority.

Can you witness an affidavit or administer an oath?

No. Those functions can require an authorised legal professional or other authorised person. We provide ordinary signature witnesses only.

Can you notarise or certify my document?

No. Notarisation and many forms of certification are distinct services with their own requirements. We do not present our independent witnesses as notaries or authorised certifiers.

What if my form says the witness must be a professional?

Then this general service may not be suitable. Follow the document's requirements. If it specifies a solicitor, doctor, accountant, notary or another particular profession or qualification, you should arrange somebody who satisfies that requirement.

What happens if I submit a request you cannot accept?

We review the information you provide against the scope of our witnessing service. A booking request does not change the requirements of your document, and we will not knowingly provide an ordinary witness for a role that requires a different authority or qualification.
