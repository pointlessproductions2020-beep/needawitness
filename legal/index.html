Frequently asked questions

Before you arrange a document witness

Can you provide witnesses for a will?
Yes, where the standard witnessing requirements are appropriate. A will in England and Wales normally requires two adult witnesses, and both must be present when the person making the will signs it. We provide two witnesses for this service from £200 plus mileage.
Can you witness a deed?
Potentially, yes. Many deeds signed by individuals require the signature to be made in the physical presence of a witness who then attests it. Your document or professional adviser should confirm the execution requirements that apply to your deed before you book.
Can you witness a deed poll?
Yes, where an ordinary witness is appropriate. GOV.UK's process for making your own unenrolled deed poll specifies two witnesses aged 18 or over. Some organisations may impose additional acceptance requirements, so you should check what the organisation receiving your deed poll needs.
Can you witness a Lasting Power of Attorney?
We may be able to witness appropriate LPA signatures subject to the restrictions that apply to the particular signer. Witnessing a signature is separate from acting as the LPA certificate provider; our standard service does not include acting as the certificate provider.
Do you provide legal advice or check whether my document is valid?
No. We provide independent witnesses, not legal advice. We do not draft, interpret or approve documents and cannot determine whether a document is legally effective. You remain responsible for confirming the witnessing requirements before signing.
Can you notarise a document or witness a statutory declaration?
Not as part of this service. If your document specifically requires a notary, solicitor, commissioner for oaths or another authorised professional, you will need to arrange the appropriate professional service.
How much does document witnessing cost?
Our current minimum is £150 for one witness or £200 for two witnesses, plus mileage. Any different charge caused by the circumstances of the appointment will be made clear before payment.
