Witness ≠ certificate provider

Two very different LPA roles

The Office of the Public Guardian distinguishes between a witness, who observes and signs for relevant signatures, and a certificate provider, who confirms that the donor understands the LPA, is acting by choice and is not being pressured or defrauded.

Current OPG guidance says witnesses must be 18 or over. The donor's witness cannot be one of the attorneys or replacement attorneys. Attorneys can witness each other's signatures, but the donor cannot witness an attorney's signature.

Need A Witness UK is offering the ordinary witness role only. We are not acting as your LPA certificate provider merely because you book this service. A certificate provider has separate eligibility and responsibilities and must either have known the donor well for at least two years or have appropriate professional skills, subject to the official restrictions.

Questions

LPA witnessing FAQs

Can you witness the donor's signature?

Yes, where you are booking us as the donor's ordinary independent witness and the official eligibility rules are met. The donor's witness must be 18 or over and cannot be one of that LPA's attorneys or replacement attorneys.

Can you witness an attorney's signature?

Yes, an impartial adult can witness an attorney signing. OPG guidance says attorneys can also witness each other, while the donor cannot witness an attorney's signature.

Are you the certificate provider?

No. This booking service supplies ordinary signature witnesses. A certificate provider has a separate job: confirming the donor understands the LPA and is acting freely, and the provider must satisfy specific eligibility rules.

Can the same person be a witness and certificate provider?

OPG guidance says an eligible certificate provider can also witness signatures. However, booking one of our ordinary witnesses does not make that person your certificate provider. You must arrange the certificate-provider role separately.

What if the donor cannot sign or make a mark?

Special rules apply. Current OPG guidance says that where someone signs on the donor's behalf under the relevant procedure, that person's signature must be witnessed by two people. Check the official guidance before booking so you request the correct number of witnesses.

Can you help me fill in or check the LPA?

No. We provide signature witnessing only. We do not complete the LPA, advise who should be appointed, assess mental capacity, provide the certificate-provider service or give legal advice.
