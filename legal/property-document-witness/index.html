England & Wales

Property deeds & HM Land Registry documents

HM Land Registry's current guidance requires deeds affecting registered land to use the correct form of execution. For an individual signing a deed in the usual way, the signature is made in the presence of a witness who attests it.

For example, HM Land Registry's guidance for a TR1 transfer provides for the transferor's signature to be witnessed, with the witness's signature, full name and address recorded. It also says the witness should be independent. Different execution requirements apply in some circumstances, including companies, attorneys and other legal persons.

We supply the witness — not the conveyancing advice. Your solicitor, conveyancer, lender or document provider should tell you how the document must be executed and who may witness it. We do not complete your property forms, select the execution clause, verify title, certify copies or give legal advice.

Questions

Property document witnessing FAQs

Can you witness a TR1 property transfer form?

Potentially, yes. HM Land Registry's TR1 guidance provides for an individual's execution to be witnessed and says the witness should be independent. You must first confirm that an ordinary independent witness is appropriate for your particular execution and follow any instructions from your conveyancer or HM Land Registry.

Can you witness a mortgage deed?

Potentially, where the lender or conveyancer's instructions permit an ordinary independent witness. Mortgage and lender requirements can differ, so confirm the requirements with the lender or conveyancer before booking.

Does the witness have to be physically present?

For the ordinary in-person deed witnessing route, yes. Our service is based on the witness being physically present with the signatory and observing the signature.

Can one person witness several signatures?

Sometimes. HM Land Registry's TR1 guidance says one person may witness more than one signature, but they must sign and complete their details for every signature witnessed. A party to the transfer cannot witness another party's signature. Confirm the requirements for your own transaction before booking.

Can you tell me how to complete my Land Registry form?

No. Need A Witness UK provides signature witnessing only. We do not complete forms or advise on ownership, transfers, mortgages, tax, execution wording or registration. Seek professional advice where you are unsure.

Can the witness come to my home or office?

Yes. Enter the signing address and postcode when booking. The system calculates the return mileage charge before you submit the request.
